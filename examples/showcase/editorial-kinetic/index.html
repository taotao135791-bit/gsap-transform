01

Type sets the tempo before motion arrives.

A page that hands the headline its weight up front needs less choreography to be felt. Letterforms carry the rhythm; motion only confirms it.

02

One accent. Held all the way through.

Burnt orange in the eyebrow, in the section number, in the cite mark. No drift, no surprise teal in the footer. The page reads as a single thought.

Restraint is the most expensive material in the room.
